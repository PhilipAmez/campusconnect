About Peerloom

Built by students who were tired of losing good study conversations in group-chat chaos.

Peerloom turns WhatsApp-style study groups into structured academic spaces — course-organized discussions, quizzes with real integrity monitoring, shared resources, and an AI study partner, all in one place.

Our story

Peerloom started with a simple, familiar frustration: every course had a WhatsApp group, and every WhatsApp group eventually turned into a wall of memes, forwarded messages, and a single pinned PDF nobody could find again the night before an exam.

Lecturers weren't much better served. Sharing a quiz meant a Google Form with no real way to know if a student had three tabs open next to it, and grading meant scrolling through a spreadsheet export with no context on where students were actually struggling.

We built Peerloom to close that gap — a platform that treats a course group as more than a chat thread: it's quizzes with genuine integrity monitoring, resources that stay organized by course, results a lecturer can actually act on, and an AI assistant that understands the difference between a casual question and an exam-week emergency.

The result is a platform students actually want to use, and lecturers can actually rely on.

How it works

From a course roster to a working quiz in minutes

Peerloom is designed so a lecturer can go from "I need a group for this course" to a graded quiz without fighting the tool along the way.

Step 1

Create a course group

A lecturer creates a group for a course or cohort, sets the institution and course code, and shares an invite — students join in seconds.

Step 2

Organize the space

Chat, shared resources, and announcements live in that same group — no separate folders, no separate app for "the important stuff."

Step 3

Publish a quiz

Build multiple-choice, short-answer, or mixed quizzes with deadlines and attempt limits, and assign them to one or more groups at once.

Step 4

Students take it, honestly

While the quiz is open, Peerloom quietly logs tab switches, copies, and pastes tied to that attempt — visible only to that quiz's lecturer.

Step 5

Grade and release results

Lecturers grade short-answer and essay responses directly against each submission, then choose whether students can review their graded answers afterward.

FAQ

Common questions

Q

Is Peerloom free to use?

Yes — creating an account, joining groups, and using core features is free. There's no payment step in the product today.

Q

Does Peerloom replace my institution's LMS?

No. Peerloom is built for the day-to-day collaboration and lightweight assessment layer — group chat, resources, and quizzes — alongside whatever official LMS your institution already uses.

Q

What does the integrity log actually track?

Only tab switches, copy actions, and paste actions during an active quiz attempt — never your camera, microphone, screen, or activity outside the quiz. See our Privacy Policy for specifics.

Q

Can students see their answers after a quiz?

Only if the lecturer explicitly turns on review for that quiz, and only once grading is complete — it's entirely the lecturer's decision, per quiz.
