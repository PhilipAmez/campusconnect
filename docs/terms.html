4. Quizzes & academic integrity

Lecturers may create quizzes with configurable time limits, attempt limits, and deadlines. While a quiz is in progress, Peerloom records a limited set of integrity signals — tab switches, copy actions, and paste actions — as described in our Privacy Policy. These signals are visible to the lecturer who owns the quiz and are intended to support, not replace, that lecturer's own academic integrity judgment.

Students agree to complete quizzes honestly and in accordance with the academic integrity policies of their own institution. Peerloom is a tool that surfaces information to lecturers — decisions about grading, discipline, or academic consequences are made by the lecturer and institution, not by Peerloom.

Note: Attempt limits and deadlines are set by each lecturer per quiz. Once your attempts are used or a deadline passes, Peerloom will not grant additional attempts automatically — contact your lecturer directly for exceptions.
